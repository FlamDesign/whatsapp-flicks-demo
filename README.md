# Flicks on WhatsApp: concept demo

Clickable concept showing Flicks as the media in three places: a business promotional message, a brand catalog, and Status. Open "Flicks formats" to jump to each one. Works on desktop (phone frame) and on a phone (full screen).

Built by Flam for partner conversations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp, Meta, Nike, Macy's, Levi's, Coach, Zara, Cadillac, Ben & Jerry's, Acer, Airbnb, LEGO or Bose. Photos via Unsplash.
